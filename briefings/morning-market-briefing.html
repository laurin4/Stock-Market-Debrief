Biggest risk

The risk that sits on this account is a December interest-rate increase meeting a fund that, on the last holdings table, was still 62.83% United States and still had Nvidia as its second-largest stock. The Campos wrap said a hike is priced for the next Federal Reserve meeting. CNBC said a quarter-point is strongly expected on 8–9 December. AWP said the market is counting on at least 25 basis points and that some economists look for 50. No FedWatch percentage was re-read this morning, so none is printed. October personal consumption expenditures, the Federal Reserve’s preferred price gauge, are due Wednesday 25 November at 8:30 a.m. Eastern, in a week when New York is closed on Thursday and shuts early on Friday. Cook’s warning, as carried by CNBC, is that waiting could mean larger increases later. A thin holiday tape can move the fund’s price without changing the holdings.

The holder’s own levels have not been hit. Friday’s 146.88 francs is 6.88 francs, about 4.7%, above 140, and 11.88 francs, about 8.1%, above 135. Those gaps are arithmetic on Vanguard’s price, not a Vanguard statistic. Selling a ten-session drawdown that has not reached either alert is a decision this note does not make.

The second risk is oil and the war, because it feeds that same inflation path. Friday’s settlement rose on renewed attack worries and pre-holiday buying, even though the week was still described as down and Hormuz flows were described as near pre-war levels. A mine claim carried by Iranian state media, and a Houthi threat to Saudi ports, are in the Reuters settlement story as statements, not as damage Reuters counted itself. Either one becoming a sustained interruption would sit on top of a portfolio that is equities only.
