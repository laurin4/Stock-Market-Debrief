Biggest risk

The risk that reaches the whole fund is still the discount rate. Producer prices rose 0.3 percent on the month and 6.5 percent on the year. Friday’s closing stories treated a December hike of at least 25 basis points as priced, with a 50-basis-point tail that those stories put near 10 or 11 percent. Monday’s Sydney snapshot put that tail at 6 percent, from 12 percent a day earlier, and said 90 basis points are priced by late next year. The 3 p.m. Friday 10-year yield was 5.152 percent, up 0.294 percentage point on the week on Dow Jones’s weekly line. The dollar index was at its highest since April 2025. Gold’s 4.6 percent weekly drop, as Monday’s Reuters note described it, is the same rate story from the other side. The Federal Reserve’s October minutes are due Wednesday, on that Reuters dispatch, which gave no clock time. Nikkei Quick’s calendar, in Japan time, has Federal Reserve chair Kevin Warsh speaking at 02:00 on Tuesday 17 November, which is Monday evening in Zurich. Reuters described that appearance as a San Francisco forum and previewed remarks on artificial intelligence. The speech had not been delivered at this writing.

The second risk is already in the top of the book and it got a cash price in Asia this morning. Nvidia is 4.55 percent of the October fund, Taiwan Semiconductor 1.68 percent, and Taiwan plus Korea plus Japan were 12.2 percent of the September country book. A messy Tuesday report would hit a top holding in the same week as a leadership vacancy. CNA reported a December Senate summons. The Financial Times report that Huang has discussed returning, and Bessent’s line that the company should look outside, are reports about a search. Reuters reported that Huang stepped down after revelations of extramarital affairs, including a relationship with a businesswoman whom United States authorities suspect of spying for China. The suspicion is an allegation in that reporting. The same Monday dispatch says President Donald Trump, President Xi Jinping and President Vladimir Putin are set to meet later in the week on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation forum in Shenzhen, on nuclear arms reduction and the United States-Israeli war with Iran. It does not give a date or a clock. A difficult combination for this fund would be a discount rate that stays high and a further break in the chip complex, because one hits the multiple on a 19.6 times portfolio and the other hits holdings the fund already owns.
