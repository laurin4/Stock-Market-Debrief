Nvidia ended 31.5% higher in the Valetkevitch close, its biggest daily percentage gain since 9 May 2003. The resignation date in the Valetkevitch copy is 12 November. Both Reuters copies describe the exit as following reports of extramarital affairs, including one involving a woman described as an alleged Chinese spy or as someone United States officials suspect of spying for China. Valle adds that Huang has not directly addressed that reporting. These are allegations carried by the wires. They are not findings. Hottest tactical

The session the market chased was the reinstatement trade. Nvidia’s 31.5% close, a 14% day for the Philadelphia semiconductor index, a Swiss Market Index led by ABB, and a Nikkei that traded as high as 59,262.57 before closing at 58,644.63 are one story: the leadership headline put a bid back under the artificial-intelligence complex that led last week’s damage. Australia’s first up-day in eight sessions fits the same tape. What is not in hand is a cash close for Korea, Taiwan, Hong Kong or Shanghai, and the only United States futures line opened this morning was Li’s 04:17 UTC mark, with the broad contract flat and the Nasdaq contract down 0.2%. A long-term holder of this fund does not need to trade that headline. The tactical fact is the speed of the reversal, and how little of it is settled once the chief-executive announcement is behind the market.

Biggest risk

For a long-term holder, the risk is treating Monday as a resolution. Huang is back in the job. The wires still carry an unresolved national-security allegation, lawmakers are on the record questioning the decision, and Valetkevitch’s own quoted investor says one large issue is out of the way and the rest still has to play out. Almost 63% of the fund is the United States, and the published Nvidia weight is six weeks old, so neither “the fund missed the rebound” nor “the fund is already repaired” can be read off the holdings page. Rates did not celebrate with the stock tape. Valle’s 2-year yield rose 7.44 basis points, and Li’s 04:17 UTC FedWatch snapshot had a December increase priced as a certainty, with around 5% odds of 50 basis points, ahead of Wednesday’s personal consumption expenditures report. Thursday’s Thanksgiving close and Friday’s early close then thin the tape. Oil settled at a three-month low on a supply story while the same article carried fresh attack claims from the Houthis, Bahrain, the Revolutionary Guards and Ukraine. Those claims are not a reason to abandon a global fund, and they are not a reason to add because one chip stock rose 31.5% in a day.
